# Reference Case Summary: CASE-2026-88192
## Account: Apex Global Logistics (ACC-100482)

### Incident Description
On September 22, 2026 at 21:14 UTC, account ACC-100482 initiated an uncharacteristic $84,500.00 international wire transfer to a newly designated beneficiary account in Eastern Europe. 

### Risk Indicators
- Amount exceeded the 30-day baseline single wire average ($6,200.00) by over 13x.
- Transaction initiated at 02:14 local time outside normal corporate business hours.
- Originating IP had zero prior historical association with the corporate treasury credentials.
- System unified risk score evaluated at 87.5 (Classification: CRITICAL).

### Analyst Resolution
Case assigned to Senior Risk Analyst Sarah Chen. Outbound wire paused under SOP-104 Section 4. Verification callback confirmed accountholder corporate controller was traveling; secondary phone authorization was completed before funds were released.
