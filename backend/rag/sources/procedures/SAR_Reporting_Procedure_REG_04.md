# Regulatory Compliance Procedure: REG-04
## Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) & SAR Filing Standards

### Legislative Authority
Enacted under FinCEN regulatory guidance and the Bank Secrecy Act for identifying and reporting suspicious transactions involving potential money laundering or terror financing.

### Evidentiary Thresholds
- Automated machine learning predictions, risk probabilities, and anomaly scores do NOT constitute sufficient sole basis for filing a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR).
- Every submitted SAR dossier must contain:
  1. Concrete transactional facts (timestamps, counterparties, dollar values, corridors).
  2. Identified anomalies with quantified deviations from accountholder baseline.
  3. Documentary record of analyst review, including callback verifications or customer inquiry attempts.
  4. Immutable audit trail timestamping the analyst's determination.

### Timelines
SAR filings must be concluded and transmitted within 30 calendar days of initial suspicious activity detection.
