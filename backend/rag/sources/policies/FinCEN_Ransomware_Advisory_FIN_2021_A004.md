# FinCEN Advisory on Ransomware and Cyber-Enabled Financial Crime (FIN-2021-A004)

## 1. Overview and Institutional Mandate
This advisory provides mandatory compliance guidance for financial institutions regarding ransomware payments, illicit virtual asset conversions, and associated cybercrime laundering typologies under the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA).

## 2. Red Flag Indicators for Ransomware and Cyber Extortion
Financial institutions must conduct immediate enhanced due diligence (EDD) when any of the following indicators are detected:
1. **Sudden Out-of-Pattern High-Value Wire**: An account holder suddenly requests an urgent, high-value wire transfer or foreign exchange to a recipient with whom they have no documented business history.
2. **Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) Intermediation**: Transaction beneficiaries linked directly or indirectly to unhosted wallets, high-risk cryptocurrency exchanges, or mixers.
3. **Executive Urgency or Secrecy**: Instructions marked as "Strictly Confidential", "Urgent Legal Settlement", or bypassing standard dual-authorization procedures.
4. **Off-Hours / Night-Time Initiation**: Wire requests submitted outside regular business hours (especially 01:00 to 05:00 UTC) with demands for immediate non-recallable settlement.

## 3. Mandatory Reporting and Escalation
- Any confirmed ransomware transaction exceeding $5,000 must result in a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filed within 30 calendar days.
- If immediate asset flight or ongoing extortion is suspected, the Compliance Officer must notify FinCEN and law enforcement within 24 hours.
